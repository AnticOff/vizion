In short

  • The website has no account, no sign-up and no form sent to a server.
  • The quote cart computes the estimate in your browser and keeps your service choices there. Nothing is sent until you send the prepared email yourself from your mail app.
  • No cookies, no audience measurement, no advertising. Fonts and libraries are hosted on the website itself.
  • Your data is never sold.

Data controller

Vizion, [legal form], [postal address], reachable at [email address]. Full details are in the legal notice.

Data processed

When you write to us

If you send a quote request or an email, we receive what your message contains: your name, your email address, your company name and size, the services and quantities you chose, and your message.

When you use the quote cart

Your choices (services, quantities, options, models added from the library) are saved in your browser’s local storage so you find them again from one page to another. They contain no personal data, never leave your device and are erased by the “Clear quote” button. The details you type (name, email, company, message) are not saved and disappear when you leave the page. Nothing reaches us until you send the prepared email.

When you become a client

Billing details, project exchanges, quotes, invoices and delivery documents.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Answering your requests and preparing a quote: steps taken at your request before a contract (Article 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Delivering the services, following the project and the delivery: performance of the contract (Article 6.1.b).
  • Invoicing and bookkeeping: legal obligation (Article 6.1.c).
  • Keeping the website secure, through the host’s technical logs: legitimate interest (Article 6.1.f).

Retention periods

  • Requests not followed by an order: [3 years] from the last contact.
  • Client data: for the duration of the business relationship, then [3 years].
  • Invoices and accounting records: 10 years, as required by the French Commercial Code.

Recipients

Your data is read by the Vizion team only. It goes through our email provider, [email provider name], and, for clients, our invoicing tool, [tool name]. It is never sold or transferred.

Hosting and logs

The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web server, GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses in technical logs for the security of the service. These logs are managed by GitHub under its privacy statement; Vizion has no access to them.

Cookies and trackers

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers: no audience measurement, no advertising, no social media buttons. Only your browser’s local storage keeps the content of your quote cart; it serves only that feature, which you use, so it does not require consent. That is why no consent banner is shown.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction, erasure, restriction of processing or portability, and object to its processing. Write to [email address]; we reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, at cnil.fr.

Changes

This policy may change, for example if the website adds a form or an analytics tool. The update date at the top of the page applies.
